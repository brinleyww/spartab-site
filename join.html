Join the team

You don't need to know anything about robots. Most of us didn't when we started.

How it works

We have a booth at Skyline's club fair every fall. Come say hi and ask whoever's there anything. After that, come to the first meeting, where we go over how the season works and what everyone does. Bring a friend if you want.

There's build, wiring, programming, CAD, scouting, photos, outreach and sponsors, and you can try more than one.

Questions people ask

Do I have to come to every meeting?
No. Come when you can. You'll get more out of it the more you show up, especially during build season (January to March).
I missed the club fair. Is it too late?
Email skyline.spartabots@gmail.com and we'll tell you when the next meeting is.
Who runs the team?
Students. Mentors help and keep things safe, but the robot and the decisions are ours.

Where you'll see us

When we aren't at competitions we try to get robots in front of people:

  • Science fairs at local elementary schools, like Cedar Trails
  • Salmon Days in Issaquah
  • The Issaquah Farmers Market in the summer
  • Local parades
  • School assemblies and freshman orientation night

If you'd like us to bring a robot to something, email us.
