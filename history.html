History

Every season since our rookie year: 18 so far, with 6 trips to the District Championship and 4 to the FIRST Championship. Results come from FIRST's event records. If something's wrong, tell us.

2018

HERMES qualified for the FIRST Championship in Houston on district points, 24th in the Pacific Northwest. We went 6-4 in quals and finished 18th of 68 in the Hopper division. 254, the Cheesy Poofs, were the first seed and picked 148 (the Robowranglers) and then us, with 3075 as the fourth robot.

The alliance won Hopper 6-0 and went through Einstein without a loss. In the finals against the Carver champions we won 379-209 and 392-146. That's still the only world title in team history.

That fall we also won Washington Girls' Generation, and in 2019 we won Peak Performance, both offseason events.
