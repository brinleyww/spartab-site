2026 · REBUILT

Diggy (Hades)

A swerve robot with a pivoting intake that feeds a conveyor and an indexer into the shooter. The shooter sets its speed from how far away the goal is, and there's an align-to-goal command so the driver doesn't have to aim by eye. For localization it used a Limelight plus a Quest headset (QuestNav).

  • Judges' Award at Bonney Lake
  • District FIRST Impact Award at Sammamish
  • Played the PNW District Championship in Cheney, 7-5 in quals

We don't have a good photo of Diggy up yet. Code: DiggyUnbound (season) and DiggyRecode (the fall rewrite).

2025 · REEFSCAPE

Ares

An elevator with an end effector for scoring coral on the reef, fed by a funnel and a slapdown intake, plus a climber. The drivers had buttons to line up on the reef poles automatically, and autos were planned in Choreo. Everything logged through AdvantageKit.

  • Judges' Award at Sammamish
  • District FIRST Impact Award at Bonney Lake
  • District Championship FIRST Impact Award (2412, the Robototes, won one too), which sent us to Houston
  • 25th of 75 in the Milstein division at the FIRST Championship, 7-3

Code: AresAbsolute

2024 · CRESCENDO

Artemis

We finished quals at Auburn 30th of 35 and still made the finals, as the second pick of the 8th alliance. Lost the final 1-2 to the 1st alliance.

  • District Event Finalist at Auburn
  • Creativity Award at Auburn
  • Alliance 3 at Sammamish, out in round 3

Code: Artemis2.0

2023 · CHARGED UP

Zeus

A first-round pick at both of our events, at Glacier Peak and at Sammamish. We didn't make the District Championship that year.

  • Alliance 6 at Glacier Peak, out in round 2
  • Alliance 5 at Sammamish, out in round 3
2022 · RAPID REACT

Chronos

Our best season since 2018. Finalists at both district events, 11-1 in quals at the District Championship (2nd seed), and finalists there too. Finished 4th in the whole Pacific Northwest in district points and went back to Houston.

  • Excellence in Engineering Award at Auburn
  • Quality Award at Bonney Lake
  • Autonomous Award and District Championship Finalist at Cheney
  • Semifinals in the Hopper division at Champs
2020 · INFINITE RECHARGE

Olympus

Captained the 6th alliance at Glacier Peak and made the finals, and won the Innovation in Control Award. That turned out to be the only event of the year. Our second event and Championship were cancelled for COVID. In 2021 we did the at-home challenges instead and picked up a Quality Award.

2019 · DESTINATION: DEEP SPACE

Khaos

Finalist at Auburn Mountainview with the Autonomous Award, Innovation in Control at Glacier Peak, then back to Houston, where we made the Hopper division finals and lost 1-2.

2018 · FIRST POWER UP

Hermes

The world champion. Picked by 254 in the Hopper division with 148 and 3075, unbeaten in the playoffs from there. The whole story is on the history page.
