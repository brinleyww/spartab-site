Spartabots 2976

FIRST Robotics from Skyline High School in Sammamish, Washington

We're a student-run robotics team at Skyline High. Every January FIRST releases a new game, and we get a few weeks to design, build and program a robot to play it. Then we take it to competitions around Washington.

The team started in 2008 in a mentor's garage. Since then we've played 18 seasons, gone to the FIRST Championship four times, and won it once, in 2018.

Since 2018 every robot has had a name from Greek mythology.

2026: REBUILT

Our 2026 robot was Hades, a shooter on a swerve drive that could line itself up with the goal.

It wasn't our best year in the standings, but we won the District FIRST Impact Award at Sammamish, which is about everything the team does and not just the robot.

In October we took Hades to Bordie Through Time in Bellevue, and now we're getting ready for 2027. Kickoff is in January.
