Sponsor the team

Your money goes to the robot and to getting students to competitions: registration, parts and tools, and travel when we qualify for the District Championship or the FIRST Championship.

It doesn't have to be money either. Machining time, materials, and mentors who know CAD, electrical or software help just as much.

Whatever you give, you'll be on this page and we'll say thank you a lot.
